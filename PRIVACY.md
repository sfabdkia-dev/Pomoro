## What stays on your device
Your tasks, activity log, settings and focus history are stored locally on your computer. They are never uploaded.

## Optional research data
Pomoro can share anonymous usage data to support research on what helps people focus.

- **Who is asked:** In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland, and where your region can't be determined, sharing is off until you choose to turn it on. Elsewhere it is on by default after a first-run notice, and you can turn it off immediately or at any time.
- **Control:** Settings > Privacy lets you turn sharing off and see exactly what would be sent.

### What is sent (once per day, for the previous day)
- A random ID generated on your device (not linked to your name, email, account or hardware)
- App version and operating system platform
- Your focus time for that day, rounded to 5 minutes, and the number of focus sessions
- Which settings are on, such as session lengths, sound mode, notifications, deadline reminders, always-on-top, start with Windows, dark mode, UI theme and daily goal
- Rounded-range counts of your tasks and recurring tasks (for example "6-20")

Days with no focus time are not sent.

### What is never sent
Task names or text, log entries, your name, email, device name, files, exact times of day, or your IP address. Events are sent without creating a person profile, and the analytics project is configured to discard IP addresses.

### Where it goes
Data is sent over HTTPS to PostHog's EU cloud (Frankfurt, Germany) and used only for research on focus and productivity tools. Results are published only in aggregate.
